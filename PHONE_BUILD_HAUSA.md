# JIDDA PHOTOSHOP — Gina APK daga waya

## Hanya mafi sauki
Za ka iya amfani da AndroidIDE domin buɗe wannan Gradle project kai tsaye a Android phone.

1. Shigar da AndroidIDE daga trusted source.
2. Extract `JiddaPhotoshopAndroidProject.zip` zuwa storage na waya.
3. Buɗe AndroidIDE → Open Project.
4. Zaɓi folder `JiddaPhotoshopAndroid` (wanda yake da `settings.gradle`).
5. Jira Gradle Sync ya gama.
6. Zaɓi **Build > Assemble Debug**.
7. Bayan an gama, APK zai kasance a:
   `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`
8. Danna APK ɗin ka install.

## Idan Gradle Sync ya nemi SDK
Daga AndroidIDE SDK/Terminal, tabbatar akwai Android SDK Platform 35 da Build Tools.

## Muhimmin bayani
Wannan build ɗin farko yana kunshe da JIDDA PHOTOSHOP Home Screen da zaɓin hoto daga Gallery. AI editing kamar Remove Object, Change Outfit da Background za a haɗa su a mataki na gaba.
